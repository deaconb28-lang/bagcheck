01 — Voice

Descriptive, never prescriptive

State what the user did. Never suggest a trade, never imply urgency. No exclamation marks, no coaching tone, no "you should." Numbers describe behavior — hold time, drawdown response, conviction decay, streaks — never a benchmark and never another person. One notification a day, and it is never a price alert.

Say this

You held through three straight down weeks. That's new for you.

Your winners: 41 days. Your losers: 6.

Never this

Consider trimming your position before earnings.

You're beating 94% of investors — keep it up!

02 — Color

Aubergine-black, not black

The canvas carries a violet undertone rather than sitting at neutral black, so a gold readout looks lit rather than merely colored. Light mode mirrors it in warm bone. Token names are legacy — --moss is gold, --signal is violet. Keep them.

03 — Semantics

Two axes, one accent per surface

Gold and violet are not a highlight pair — they are two readings of the same person. Never let two saturated fills compete in one view; when both belong on a surface, the secondary drops to --ink2.

● Gold — discipline

Rules kept, contributions made, drawdowns held, positive P&L. Every primary CTA.

● Violet — exposure

Trade count, speculative weight, percentile, any comparison against other people.

● Clay — negative P&L

Nothing else. Warnings, errors, and empty states stay in ink.

Share cards and Wrapped viewers stay aubergine-black in both modes.

05 — Data idiom

One idiom per statement

Each must stay legible at 200px wide. Full equity curves live on Portfolio and nowhere else. No decorative gradients, no emoji, no icon sets, and never four identical sparklines in a row.

Segment ring — a quarter's discipline

63 segments, one per trading day. Filled when you stayed inside your rules.

Day-cell grid

The same data unrolled where the surface is wider than it is tall.

Dot scatter — the gap is the insight

Entries by hour. The cluster after 2pm is where the losses live.

Distribution — your marker on the tail

Everyone invested through the same window. You are the violet mark.

Decay line — conviction over time

How long your stated conviction survives contact with a drawdown.

Contribution grid — current cell lit

Deposits by week. The lit cell is the one in progress.

08 — Surfaces, motion, minimums

The rules that keep it consistent

✓ 16–22px radii, 1px hairlines, 26–34px padding

No shadows in-app. Flex and grid with gap — never margin-spaced inline siblings.

✓ 640px reading column, rails on desktop

Single column with bottom tabs on mobile, breaking at 900px. Err toward whitespace.

✓ Ease-out, nothing over 500ms

Entrances stagger ~80ms. Count-ups run on setInterval, and the real value is the default render so numbers are correct if the tween never starts.

✕ No decorative gradients or emoji

No icon sets, and never four identical sparklines. One idiom per statement.

✓ 44px hit targets, 24px slide floor, 12pt print

Honour prefers-reduced-motion everywhere.

✕ Never hardcode a palette hex

Always var(--*). Both modes are declared on html; dark is :root.
