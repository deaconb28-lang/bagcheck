Fonts vendored into this repository
===================================

Every face Supercruise renders is served from this directory rather than fetched
from a CDN. That is a build-reliability decision first — `next/font/google`
downloads its files during `next build`, and one failed fetch takes the whole
build with it — and a privacy decision second: nothing about a reader is sent
to a font host, which is what lets /legal/privacy say no third party is in the
render path.

Refresh with `npm run fonts` (scripts/vendor-fonts.mjs). Only the latin and
latin-extended cuts are kept; the whole set is about 450kB.

  space-grotesk-*.woff2       Space Grotesk       SIL OFL 1.1
  playfair-display-*.woff2    Playfair Display    SIL OFL 1.1
  public-sans-*.woff2         Public Sans         SIL OFL 1.1
  jetbrains-mono-*.woff2      JetBrains Mono      SIL OFL 1.1
  anton-*.woff2               Anton               SIL OFL 1.1
  outfit-*.woff2 / .ttf       Outfit              SIL OFL 1.1
  general-sans-*.woff2        General Sans        ITF Free Font Licence

The .ttf is for the share-card renderer: Satori parses TTF, OTF and WOFF, and
cannot read WOFF2.

General Sans is by the Indian Type Foundry, from Fontshare. The ITF Free Font
Licence permits use, including self-hosting, for personal and commercial work
at no cost; the fonts may not be resold on their own.
https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl
